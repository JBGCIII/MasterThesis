

Replication Note:
Full re-estimation of all 25 models is heavily reliant on 
computing capacity. Depending on your hardware it might take
anything from between eight hours to even a day! I do not
expected you to run the model again and have thus provided
the already estimated models as a rds. files you can load
in for the analysis!

To replicate the tables/figures using pre-computed results:
Download model_outputs.zip from [Link Here].
Unzip the contents into the 4_Output_Analysis/ directory.
Run main_analysis.R.
If you wish to re-estimate the models from scratch you can still do it!
I, however, would suggest to avoud running the narrative one as it takes
the longest. Narrative models not included you could shorten the time
substantially. I used 7 Cores for my estimation, you might have more,
or a secret DARPA super computer.
